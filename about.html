Our Story

North Star Bakery began with a simple idea: good food has a way of bringing people together. What started with small batches of bread and pastries grew into a neighborhood bakery where customers can stop in, slow down, and leave with something freshly made.

We want our bakery to feel familiar, whether you visit every week or you are walking through the door for the first time. Around here, we believe there is always a reason to celebrate, even if that reason is simply making it through a long Monday.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We focus on quality ingredients and thoughtful preparation. Whenever possible, we choose ingredients from local and regional suppliers and prepare our products in small batches.

Fresh ingredients, careful baking, and dependable quality are at the heart of what we do. We believe simple recipes made with care can create the kind of food people come back for.

Made for Our Neighborhood

North Star Bakery is built around the people we serve. From a regular morning pastry to a cake that becomes part of a family celebration, we are proud to be there for both the ordinary days and the big ones.
